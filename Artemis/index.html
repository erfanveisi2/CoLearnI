Mission Phases

Phase 01
▲

Launch

SLS lifts off from Kennedy Space Center, carrying Orion beyond Earth's atmosphere in under eight minutes.

Phase 02
➜

Trans-Lunar Injection

Upper stage fires to send Orion on a trajectory toward the Moon, a quarter-million miles away.

Phase 03
◯

Lunar Orbit

Orion enters a near-rectilinear halo orbit, positioning the crew for rendezvous with the lunar lander.

Phase 04
●

Landing

Astronauts descend to the lunar south pole — the first humans on the Moon since Apollo 17.

Phase 05
↺

Return

Orion separates, performs a skip re-entry through Earth's atmosphere, and splashes down in the Pacific.
